Chant the Heart Sutra in Mandarin — with pinyin, and a page that follows your voice

Twelve Buddhist sutras and mantras as chanted in Chinese temples, with pinyin above every character. Chant aloud and the page listens, highlighting the line you are on so you never lose your place. Free, no sign-up.

“When Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva was practicing the profound prajñāpāramitā…” — the opening of the Heart Sutra. Note the traditional temple readings: 般若 is bō rě, not bān ruò.

Who it is for

  • Children and grandchildren of Chinese Buddhist families who want to chant with their parents but never learned to read the characters.
  • Students in temple chanting classes who need to practise at home between sessions.
  • English-speaking practitioners who want to learn the Mandarin liturgy as it is actually chanted.

How to use it (the page is in Chinese)

  1. Open a text, for example the Heart Sutra. Every character has its pinyin above it.
  2. Tap the button labelled 听我念 (“listen to me chant”) and allow microphone access when your browser asks.
  3. Chant aloud at your own pace. The current line lights up and the page scrolls with you. If you skip ahead or repeat, it catches up.
  4. Prefer not to use the microphone? Use auto-scroll or turn pages by hand. You can also add a wooden-fish (muyu) beat or background music.

Works on iPhone, iPad, Android and desktop browsers. According to the site, voice recognition runs on your device and your voice is not uploaded.

Questions

Do I need to read Chinese?

No. Follow the pinyin. The page interface is in Chinese, so the steps above tell you where to tap.

Is it free?

Yes. No account needed.

Can I hear how it should sound first?

Yes. Chanted and recited recordings are on the sutra recordings page and on our YouTube channel @qiucaiqifu.
